# Design System: Omkar Kulkarni Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Shader Studio"**

The page is a studio portal. A single full-screen WebGL field, silk ribbons of fine strands running lilac into ember over a deep void, is the ground of everything; the work floats over it as thin, condensed, uppercase display type, small tracked labels, plain sentence-case body, and a few solid ink panels edged in 1px mist hairlines. The identity is carried by the moving surface and the huge condensed type, with very little else on screen. This world replaced an earlier periodic-table world that the owner rejected as cluttered, so restraint is the operating principle: sleek and clean beats dense and decorated.

Density is low and the rhythm is generous: sections are separated by a fluid 6 to 11rem band, content sits in a 1480px measure, and the left of the field is kept near-void so type always reads on quiet ground. Interaction is where the richness lives: the field leans toward a hovered project's hue, project cards tilt and their architecture diagrams flow faster, skill groups illuminate while the rest dim, credentials flip to their certificate and open into a full-screen sheet through a circular clip-path, arrows page through that sheet, and the whole page scrolls with inertia. Every one of these is kept by the owner's instruction, and every one has a calm fallback.

Confirmed rejections: no glass blur, no glow halos, no monospace, no gradients on text at rest (the two exceptions are the transient Molten pour and the Molten Name, below), and nothing from the previous site's look (JetBrains Mono, blueprint console, cyan). No headline numbers anywhere: figures live inside sentences, tinted, never enlarged.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One living shader field as the ground of the whole page; panels float over it, never replace it.
- Archivo variable at condensed width (wdth 64), light weight (350), uppercase, for every display line.
- Small uppercase labels tracked wide (0.16 to 0.34em) as the second voice; body stays plain sentence case.
- Lilac is the voice of the system; ember appears in the interface only when something is hovered, pressed, current, or focused, with the Resume outline as the one exception.
- Solid ink panels with 1px mist hairlines; depth is offset shadow on hover only.
- Motion everywhere, all of it dropped or frozen under reduced motion.

### Project page family
The four GitHub Pages project sites (Experiment Decision Engine, ReelRank, Fieldnote, Casefile) are the same system in long-form: void ground, the same ribbon field, Archivo condensed display in display-ink, tracked labels, lilac as the accent, ember only on hover, and the same diagram vocabulary with mint and soft red as outcome tones. Their look lives in two master files, `/Volumes/Crucial_1TB/projects/project-sites/_theme/theme.css` (tokens and components) and `/Volumes/Crucial_1TB/projects/project-sites/_theme/field.js` (the shader, with no hover lean, its quiet mask moved further right, and a calmer scroll falloff), copied into each site folder. Edit the masters and recopy; a change to this file's tokens should be mirrored there.

## Colors

A cold indigo void lit by one cool accent (lilac) and one hot signal (ember), with mist-tinted type and hairlines.

### Primary
- **Studio Lilac** (lilac): the system's voice. Fills the primary actions as a translucent wash (lilac-wash at rest, lilac-wash-hover on hover), marks the hairline bullets of experience points, the live status dot, text selection, the hover edge of credential tiles, accent diagram nodes, the resting marker of the analytics project family, and the lilac half of every shader ribbon.
- **Lilac Readout** (lilac-text): the legible lilac for text on the void. Used for inline numerals, company names, credential meta, the dashed in-progress tag, and the accented edges of project diagrams.

### Secondary
- **Ember Signal** (ember): the hot end of the shader ribbons and the interface's state colour. In chrome and type it marks hover, pressed, current and focus: the active nav dot, the focus ring (2px outline), a pressed skill filter, a lit skill group's rule and title, hovered sheet controls and contact icons, the hover edge of the hero primary action. It is also the hover and active accent of the AI-engineering project family, so the field leans that way when one is hovered.

### Neutral
- **Void** (void): the page ground beneath the shader, the solid sheet background, and the scrolled header bar. The preview plate inside project cards is void at 60%.
- **Ink** (ink): the opaque body of panels (panel is ink at 86%, diagram nodes ink at 92%) and the portrait matte.
- **Night** (night): scrollbar thumb and the deepest raised tone.
- **Starlight** (text): primary text, card titles, wordmark, nav current state.
- **Haze** (text-2): body copy in cards and lists, taglines, secondary values.
- **Dusk** (text-3): resting labels, resting nav links, scroll rail captions, diagram subs, captions and plain edges.
- **Mist** (mist): the cold edge colour: hover border on filters, the close control's ring, the scroll rail knob, and the resting marker of the AI-engineering project family. Hairlines are mist at 13% (line) and 28% (line-strong).
- **Display Ink** (display-ink): the soft lilac-white of the hero name, section headlines, the contact heading and the sheet title.
- **Selection Text** (selection-text): white, used only for selected text on a lilac selection.
- **Certificate Paper** (certificate-paper): the light mat behind certificate images only, on the credential tile's back face and in the credential sheet.

### Diagram tones
- **Outcome Mint** (diagram-good) marks the approved or success outcome in an architecture diagram (Ship, Cited answer, Human gate): mint stroke over a mint 8% fill, mint title.
- **Outcome Red** (diagram-bad-stroke, diagram-bad-text) marks a failure outcome (Blocked, No ship): soft red stroke at 70%, #ff9d9d title.
These are semantic diagram tones only. They never colour buttons, borders, labels or any other interface element.

### Named Rules
**The Ember Rule.** Ember in the interface marks a state, never a resting surface: hover, pressed, current, focus. At rest, chrome is mist, dusk, starlight or lilac. Project families carry two colours: a resting marker that is never ember (lilac for analytics, mist for AI engineering) and an accent (lilac or ember) used only on hover and active states. The shader's ribbons and the per-project flow-line fallback drawings are world materials and keep their family hue. The single sanctioned resting-ember element is the Resume outline (header pill and hero action), because the resume is the primary exit; it sets no pattern for any other button.

**The Tinted Numeral Rule.** Every figure in running text is set in lilac-text at weight 650 with tabular numerals, in place, at the size of its sentence. A number is never promoted to its own line, its own size, or its own card.

**The True Photo Rule.** A real photograph is shown as taken, with no dimming or colour wash (the user found the graded portrait too dark, 2026-11-26). It sits in an ink matte frame; the type around it carries the palette.

**The Hairline Rule.** Separation is a 1px mist line (line for dividers inside content, line-strong for the edges of panels, tiles and pills). No filled dividers, no thick rules.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo Variable (with Archivo, Helvetica Neue, Arial)
**Body Font:** Archivo Variable (same family)
**Label/Mono Font:** none; labels are Archivo uppercase with wide tracking. No monospace anywhere.

**Character:** One family carries every voice by moving along its width and tracking axes: condensed and light for monumental display, normal width and tracked wide for small labels, normal width and plain for reading. The contrast between a 9.5rem condensed name and a 0.72rem label tracked at 0.24em is the system's typographic signature.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (350, wdth 64, clamp 4.25 to 9.5rem, line-height 0.88, uppercase, display-ink): the hero name only, one word per masked line.
- **Headline** (350, wdth 64, clamp 3 to 6.25rem, line-height 0.9, uppercase, display-ink): section headings, rising word by word. The same recipe sets the contact heading larger (clamp 3.5rem, 9vw, 8.5rem) and the certificate sheet title smaller (clamp 2.75rem, 5.6vw, 5.25rem).
- **Title** (350, wdth 64, uppercase, starlight): role titles in Experience (clamp 2.2rem, 3.6vw, 3.25rem) and institutions in Education (clamp 2rem, 3.4vw, 3rem), sticky beside their entries on desktop.
- **Title Sans** (500, normal width, clamp 1.5 to 2.1rem, line-height 1.2, sentence case): the publication title inside its panel.
- **Card Title** (550, 1rem, tracked 0.16em, uppercase): project names on cards.
- **Lede** (400, clamp 1.08rem, 1.4vw, 1.25rem, line-height 1.7, starlight): the line that stands in for a certificate without an image in the sheet.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, line-height 1.6 to 1.7, tabular numerals): sentence case, held to 60 to 68ch. Card summaries run a step smaller (0.98rem, line-height 1.65, 60ch) and are one or two sentences.
- **Status Line** (550, 0.95rem, line-height 1.4): the hero Currently readout entries.
- **Body Small** (400, 0.875rem): credit lines, link values, sub-lines of the status readout.
- **Label** (500, 0.72rem, tracked 0.24em, uppercase, dusk): section asides, sheet labels, nav links (tracked 0.2em), footer. Buttons use 0.7 to 0.75rem / 600 / 0.2 to 0.24em; pills and the family label 0.68rem; the in-progress tag 0.62rem / 600 / 0.2em; scroll rail captions 0.6rem / 500 / 0.24em, set vertically; the wordmark 0.8rem / 500 / 0.34em.
- **Diagram type** (SVG user units, so it scales with the diagram): node titles 9.5px / 600 / 0.06em uppercase, node subs 8.5px in dusk, captions 8px / 500 / 0.18em uppercase in dusk.

### Named Rules
**The Condensed Display Rule.** Display lines are Archivo at wdth 64, weight 350, uppercase, letter-spacing -0.01em, line-height 0.88 to 0.9. Never bold, never at normal width, never with a gradient fill at rest. The only gradient a display line ever wears is the transient Molten pour, which cools back to display-ink.

**The Two-Voice Rule.** Uppercase belongs to display lines and tracked labels. Body copy and summaries stay sentence case at normal tracking.

## Layout

A single column of sections inside a 1480px measure with a fluid gutter (clamp 1.25 to 4.5rem). Sections are spaced by a fluid band (clamp 6 to 11rem) below each, and every large section opens with a headline on a hairline (padding-bottom 1.25rem, margin-bottom clamp 2 to 3.5rem) with an optional label aside on the right.

The fixed header is 4.5rem tall: tracked wordmark, a long fading hairline, tracked section links (from 1100px), and the Resume pill. It is transparent over the hero and becomes solid void with a bottom hairline once scrolled. A vertical scroll rail (from 1280px) sits on the right edge: a 1px track 11rem tall, a hollow mist knob, and vertical captions for the current section.

Desktop patterns are asymmetric splits with a sticky lead. The hero has three layouts. Phones stack the round portrait, name, tagline (sentence case, 1.05rem body text rather than tracked capitals), actions as an equal two-column pair, then the Currently readout. From 760px it is two columns, `minmax(0,1fr) minmax(14rem,18rem)`: name, tagline and actions on the left; the 4:5 portrait plate and Currently on the right. From 1200px it is one row of three blocks sharing a single centre line (`align-items: center`): name and actions on the left, the portrait plate in the middle, and tagline above Currently on the right (tagline tracked, under a strong hairline, in the primary text colour; Currently under a hairline). Right padding clamp(1.5rem, 5vw, 5.5rem) keeps it clear of the scroll rail. Experience runs 5fr / 7fr with the title sticky at 6.5rem, education 5fr / 6fr, credentials 3fr / 9fr, the certificate sheet 6fr / 5fr with the lead sticky at 7rem. Projects run two up from 900px; skills four up from 1100px (two from 720px); credential tiles four up from 1100px and auto-fill at 13rem below. On phones everything stacks: the portrait becomes a 6.5rem circle above the name, and the two hero actions sit side by side in a two-column grid.

The left of the viewport is the type side; the shader keeps it near-void (its mask rises from 38% to 72% of the width), so headings and body always read on quiet ground.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from the field and from opacity, not from stacked shadows. The shader sits at z-index -1 behind a transparent body; panels float over it as solid ink at 86% (never blurred glass), so the field shows only faintly at their edges. Inside a project card, the preview plate steps down to void at 60% to read as an inset. Surfaces are flat at rest. One shadow exists, an offset and blurred drop with no colour.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lift** (`0 24px 48px -24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7), 0 2px 6px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.3)`): a project card on hover or focus-within, and the certificate image inside the credential sheet.

### Named Rules
**The Flat-At-Rest Rule.** Nothing casts a shadow at rest. Lift appears only as a response to hover or focus, and it is black, never tinted.

**The Solid Panel Rule.** Panels are ink at 86% or solid void. No backdrop-filter, no frosted glass, no coloured halo behind a surface.

## Shapes

Corners are small and graduated by scale: 2px on the focus ring, 3px (inset) for the photo inside the portrait matte, 4px (control) for rectangular actions and diagram nodes, 6px (card) for project cards, credential tiles, the publication panel and the desktop portrait plate, 8px (panel) for the single contact card, 10px on the scrollbar thumb. Everything small and secondary is a full pill (999px): the header Resume, filters, sheet controls, the in-progress tag. Circles (50%) are reserved for dots and icons: the nav current dot (5px), the status dot (7px), the project family dot (6px), contact icon rings (2.75rem), the scroll knob, and the mobile portrait.

Edges are always 1px. A dashed 1px border means in progress (the Now and In progress tags) or, in a diagram, a muted node; never decoration. Masks are part of the shape language: display words rise out of overflow-hidden masks, the portrait develops in through an inset clip, and the certificate sheet opens as a circle clip from its tile.

## Components

### Buttons
Tactile but quiet: rectangles with a 4px corner, uppercase tracked labels, an icon at the edge.
- **Shape:** gently squared (4px), min-height 3.5rem in the hero, 3 to 3.25rem in cards, panels and sheets.
- **Primary (Explore, Read on IEEE Xplore, card Website):** a translucent lilac wash (26%) with a 1px lilac border and starlight text. Hover deepens the wash to 42% and turns the border to the accent (ember in the hero and on AI cards). Pressed nudges the hero buttons down 1px.
- **Neutral outline (card GitHub, sheet Verify credential):** a line-strong outline with starlight text. On a card, hover takes the family accent for border and text; in the sheet, hover fills lilac with void text.
- **Resume (hero and header):** ember outline and ember text, filling ember with void text on hover. The header version is a 2.5rem pill. This is the one sanctioned resting-ember element under the Ember Rule, reserved for the resume as the primary exit; no other button takes an ember outline at rest.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the shader field as the ground of every surface, with the left of the viewport near-void behind type.
- **Do** set display lines in Archivo at wdth 64, weight 350, uppercase, line-height 0.88 to 0.9, revealed by a masked rise (y 110% to 0, 1s, ease-out, 0.07s word stagger).
- **Do** run every figure in a sentence through the inline-numeral treatment: lilac-text, weight 650, tabular, at the sentence's size.
- **Do** keep ember for hover, pressed, current and focus in chrome and type; at rest use mist, dusk, starlight or lilac.
- **Do** float content on ink at 86% with a 1px line-strong edge, and reach for Lift only on hover or focus.
- **Do** design the unlit state: dim to 32% and keep it readable rather than hiding it.
- **Do** grade real photographs into the palette (dimmed, slightly desaturated, under a lilac-to-void multiply wash) so the display type leads the fold.
- **Do** keep a project card to its diagram, name, a one or two sentence summary, and two actions (GitHub, Website).
- **Do** mark in-progress items with a dashed 1px lilac-text pill, and pair every colour signal with text or shape.
- **Do** use the shared easing (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)) at 200ms for colour and border changes and 480ms for layout, opacity and shadow.
- **Do** give every motion a calm path: under reduced motion or still mode, the field freezes, diagram edges stop, inertial scroll is skipped, entrances are dropped and the sheet fades in 0.15s.

### Don't:
- **Don't** feature a big headline number for any project, role or credential; numbers stay inside sentences, tinted, never enlarged.
- **Don't** put ember on a resting button, border, label or icon; the Resume outline is the one exception, and family markers at rest are lilac or mist.
- **Don't** use outcome mint or outcome red outside a diagram node.
- **Don't** use backdrop blur, frosted glass, glow halos or coloured shadows.
- **Don't** use a monospace face, or the previous site's blueprint console look and cyan accent.
- **Don't** fill text with a gradient, except the scroll-linked Molten pour on headings (which cools back to plain type) and the Molten Name in the hero.
- **Don't** add a busy grid of tiles, a grid background, or more panels than the content needs; the identity is the field and the type, not containers.
- **Don't** set body copy in uppercase or tracked wide; that voice belongs to labels.
